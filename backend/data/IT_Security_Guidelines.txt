Enterprise IT & Information Security Policy
OFFICIAL POLICY DIRECTIVE
Section 1: Authentication and Passwords
Passwords must contain at least 12 characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one numerical
digit, and one special character (!@#$%^&*). Passwords must be updated every 90 days. Multi-Factor
Authentication (MFA) via authenticator app is mandatory across all internal portals.
Section 2: Clean Desk and Device Locking
Laptops  and  workstations  must  be  locked  manually  whenever  left  unattended.  Screens  will  lock
automatically after 5 minutes of idle inactivity. Storing or writing credentials on physical sticky notes or
unencrypted text documents is strictly prohibited and subject to audit penalties.
Section 3: Software Installation & Endpoint Control
Employees  are  not  granted  local  administrator  access  on  company-issued  hardware.  All  third-party
desktop  software,  command-line  utilities,  browser  plugins,  and  external  developer  tools  must  be
requested and whitelisted through the internal IT Service Desk ticketing portal.
Compliance Notice: This document is subject to periodic governance review. Any deviations or exceptional approvals must
be formally documented through the compliance workflow before execution.
