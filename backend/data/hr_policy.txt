HR POLICY

1. Casual Leave: Employees get 12 casual leaves per year.
2. Medical Leave: Employees are eligible for 10 medical leaves per year.
3. Travel Reimbursement: Travel claims must be submitted within 15 days of travel.
4. Internet Usage: Employees must not access non-work-related websites during office hours.
5. Attendance: Employees must mark daily attendance before 9:30 AM.
6. Code of Conduct: Respect colleagues, maintain confidentiality, and avoid harassment.
7. Performance Review: Annual performance reviews are held every December.
